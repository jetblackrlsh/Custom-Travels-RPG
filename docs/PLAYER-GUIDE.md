# Player guide

Assign +3, +2, +2, +1, 0, −1 to Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. Use every value exactly once. These are modifiers, not full ability scores. There are no levels, XP, or progression.

These allowances are maximums. You can choose fewer abilities. A character with no spells has no usable charge pool. Everyone also has training with their selected starting weapon. Additional proficiencies can be skills, tools, individual weapons, or weapon categories. Not every adventure uses every selection.

Checks roll a real d20, add the named ability modifier, and add +2 if the relevant proficiency applies. Spell checks use the spell's named ability plus +2. A total equal to or greater than the target succeeds. This compact ruleset does not add critical-hit/fumble rules. The result journal gives the complete arithmetic.

Each spell attempt costs one shared charge, including a failed roll. No charges return merely by saving, closing, or reopening. A new adventure run starts fresh. Scene-specific one-use actions are identified by the author; failed attempts may change the situation or close that option.

## Nine abilities

- **Guiding Bolt:** Wisdom spell attack vs AC, 8 fixed damage on a hit.
- **Shocking Grasp:** Intelligence touch attack vs AC, 5 fixed damage on a hit and prevents the immediate counterattack. Its successful effect is defined here; the reference tutorial supplied only a miss.
- **Magic Missile:** automatic 5 fixed damage against an eligible target; authored special-resolution scenes may instead use its narrative effect.
- **Shield of Faith:** prevent the next damaging event in the current encounter; unused protection ends on leaving the scene.
- **Command:** Wisdom +2 against a scene DC to compel a brief supported action.
- **Sleep:** Intelligence +2 against a scene DC to put an eligible creature to sleep.
- **Comprehend Languages:** reveal supported unfamiliar speech or writing without a roll.

- **Giant:** Constitution +2 against the scene DC, for a brief height shortcut or one enhanced strike.
- **Illusory Duplicate:** Wisdom +2 against the scene DC, for a two-presence puzzle or one attack protected from its counterattack.

Each requires an applicable authored opportunity and a charge. The pre-adventure screen shows whether your selected abilities have opportunities at all, not a promise that every route will encounter them. The author chooses eligible targets, consequences, and alternatives. Knowing a spell does not unlock arbitrary free-text actions.

Starting weapons use Strength, or Dexterity for ranged weapons; finesse options use the higher of Strength/Dexterity. The first adventure gives an ordinary attack 4 fixed damage, independent of weapon appearance. Damage is disclosed per option; enemy damage can be fixed or rolled.

## Portability

Export a character to preserve their build, background, appearance recipe, weapon, and abilities. Export a save to preserve a particular adventure run, including HP, charges, flags, Ink position, and journal. Saves require the matching adventure ID and version. Character exports are not tied to one adventure. No uploaded portrait is needed: all appearance parts live in the bundled core asset set.

Local browser storage may be disabled or isolated for file URLs; exported files remain the reliable backup. An imported adventure pack is authored content, not a security-audited plugin: use packs from sources you trust. No adventure file should request network access or contain external assets.

Adventures may grant temporary ability charges as a story reward. These are displayed separately, spent before eligible personal charges, saved with the adventure, and never added to the exported character. New ability IDs require engine 0.2.0 or later; older engines reject unknown IDs rather than silently changing them.
